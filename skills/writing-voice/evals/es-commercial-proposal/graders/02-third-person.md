Look only at the proposal, not at the notes after the line '---'. FAIL only if the proposal addresses the client in the second person ('ustedes', 'vos', 'tú', or verb forms such as 'pueden cancelar' or 'podés' aimed at the client). Naming the client in the third person ('Estudio Rivas puede cancelar', 'el equipo de Estudio Rivas') is what is expected. Otherwise PASS.
