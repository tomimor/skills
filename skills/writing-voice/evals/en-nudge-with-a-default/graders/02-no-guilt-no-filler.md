Look only at the nudge, not at the notes after the line '---'. FAIL only if the nudge contains guilt or filler: for example "just following up", "I know you're busy", "sorry to bother you", "circling back", "I hope you're doing well", or a remark about her not having replied. A plain "a quick nudge on the kickoff" is fine. Otherwise PASS.
