Look only at the email (title, subject, preview and body), not at the notes after the line '---'. The brief gave exactly these facts: a filter by DFE status in the clients table; an Excel export of the monotributo report with a category column; a fix for the CCMA balance, which did not update when a client had more than one CUIT; the filters were requested by several firms over WhatsApp. FAIL only if the email states something else as fact: an instruction or reassurance about what the reader must do, a named DFE state, a number or a date, a promise (such as 'we read them all'), a reason for prioritizing, or a made-up person or product name. These are fine and are not inventions: describing the plain effect of a listed change (a filter lets you see a subset without going one by one; an export gives you a file with that column), [Completar: ...] placeholders, a greeting, the thanks to the firms, an invitation to write, and a sign-off. Otherwise PASS.
