# Worked examples

Before-and-after pairs, one per register, with the reason for each change. Names, companies, numbers, and product facts are invented, so copy the move and never the claim. The order of the fixes is the same every time: facts first, then where the answer sits, then tone, then mechanics.

## English email: bad news

**Before**

```text
Hi Laura,

I hope this email finds you well! I just wanted to reach out to touch base
regarding the proposal we discussed. Unfortunately, due to some unforeseen
circumstances on our end, we are experiencing a slight delay — but rest
assured we are working diligently to deliver it as soon as possible.

Please don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.

Best regards,
Tomas
```

**After**

```text
Hi Laura,

The proposal will reach you on Thursday, two days later than I promised.
We are still waiting for one pricing input, and I'd rather send it complete.

If Thursday is too late for your review, tell me and I'll send the current
draft today.

Sorry for the delay, and thanks for your patience!

Best,
Tomas
```

- **Answer first.** The new date is the first sentence. The original never gave one.
- **The reason, once.** "Unforeseen circumstances" became the actual cause.
- **A way out.** The reader can choose the draft today over the full version on Thursday.
- **Cut:** the opener, "touch base", "rest assured", "don't hesitate". None of them told Laura anything.

## Spanish email: saying no to a customer

**Before**

```text
Estimada Carla,

¡Muchas gracias por tu consulta! Lamentablemente, en este momento no contamos
con esa funcionalidad dentro de nuestra plataforma integral. Sin embargo,
queremos que sepas que tu feedback es sumamente valioso para nosotros y lo
tendremos en cuenta para futuras actualizaciones.

¡No dudes en contactarnos ante cualquier otra consulta!

Saludos cordiales
```

**After**

```text
Hola Carla!

Hoy no tenemos la exportación por cliente. Podemos hacerla, pero necesitaría
un presupuesto aparte.

La razón es que cada estudio la pide con columnas distintas, así que es un
desarrollo a medida y no un ajuste del plan actual.

Si te interesa, armo una propuesta con costo y fecha y la vemos en una
llamada corta, sin ningún tipo de compromiso.

Quedo a disposición.

Saludos,
Tomás.
```

- **The no comes first and is plain,** followed at once by what is possible.
- **"La razón es que..."** gives Carla something she can repeat to her partner.
- **A path with no pressure:** a proposal, a short call, no commitment.
- **Register:** "Hola Carla!" and voseo in place of "Estimada" and "no dudes en contactarnos". "Tu feedback es sumamente valioso" promised nothing, so it went.

## Spanish product update: feature to benefit

**Before**

```text
🚀 ¡Nueva funcionalidad! Hemos implementado un potente sistema de filtros
avanzados sobre el campo `estado_dfe` que te permitirá llevar tu gestión al
siguiente nivel. ¡Y mucho más!
```

**After**

```text
Ahora podés filtrar tus clientes por estado de DFE. En vez de abrir uno por
uno, ves en una sola lista quiénes tienen una notificación sin leer.
```

- **What changed, what you gain.** One sentence each, in that order.
- **The reader's words.** The field name and the backticks are the builder's language. "DFE" is the reader's own acronym, so it stays and goes unexplained.
- **Cut:** "potente", "avanzados", "al siguiente nivel", "y mucho más", and the rocket.

## Spanish pitch line: nouns to verbs

**Before**

```text
Somos una plataforma integral de gestión para estudios.
```

**After**

```text
Sincronizamos los datos de toda tu cartera todos los días, así dejás de
descargarlos cliente por cliente.
```

- A pitch line starts with a verb in the first person plural: what we do, then what the reader stops doing.
- "Plataforma", "solución", and "ecosistema" name a category. The listener still doesn't know what happens on Monday morning.

## Spanish proposal: the offer first

**Before**

```text
Estimados,

Nos complace presentarles nuestra propuesta de valor. Norte es una solución
integral que les permitirá optimizar su operación. A continuación detallamos
las características de la plataforma. Al final encontrarán la inversión.
```

**After**

```text
Proponemos que Estudio Ferrer sume Norte por **$320.000 mensuales**, sin
lock-in, con 6 horas de soporte técnico incluidas para integrar la API.
```

- The first paragraph is the whole offer: who, what, the price in bold, and the terms that matter. The detail comes after, for whoever keeps reading.
- A proposal is a document, so it has no greeting and no exclamation marks. It names the client in the third person.
- "La inversión" is a price. Call it "Precio" and put the number next to it.

## Weekly update: who can now do what

**Before**

```text
Member management improvements
We've enhanced the admin experience with robust new capabilities.
```

**After**

```text
Admins invite and remove members from the dashboard
Dashboard
Send an invite by email, or remove a member from the same screen. A removal
that would leave the team without an owner is refused.
```

- The title says who can now do what, in the present tense. A reader who only reads titles still knows what shipped.
- The body adds how it works and the guardrail. The tag on the second line names the product area.

## Slack: first reply in a channel shared with a client

**Before**

```text
Hi team! 👋 Thanks so much for flagging this!! We sincerely apologize for any
inconvenience this may have caused. It looks like this might possibly be
related to an edge case that wasn't covered in the original scope, but we're
not 100% sure yet. We'll look into it ASAP and circle back!
```

**After**

```text
Thanks @Dana for the detailed write-up! I'm taking point on our side, and
@Lead is looking at the issue and both PRs now.

Is anything using this code live, or about to go live?

I'll post an update here before the end of the day.
```

- **No guess about cause or scope** in a shared channel before the team has agreed internally. The original speculated about both in public.
- **One question,** the one that decides how urgent this is.
- **"Before the end of the day"** in place of "ASAP" and "circle back".
- **An owner by name.**

## Spanish post: explaining a concept

**Before**

```text
En el vertiginoso mundo de la tecnología, la tokenización no es solo una
moda: es una revolución que está transformando el panorama financiero.
¿El resultado? Un cambio de paradigma sin precedentes. 🚀
```

**After**

```text
Tokenizar un activo se parece a escanear un documento, con una diferencia.

Si escaneás un título de propiedad, tenés una foto. Si lo tokenizás, tenés
algo que se puede transferir, dividir y usar como garantía, con reglas que
se ejecutan solas.

No sirve para todo (y está perfecto). Sirve cuando varias partes que no se
conocen necesitan confiar en el mismo registro.
```

- **One analogy from daily life,** then back to the real thing.
- **The limit is stated,** which is what makes the claim before it believable.
- **Cut:** the scene-setting opener, "no es solo... es...", the self-answered question, "revolución", "cambio de paradigma", and the emoji. After the cut, nothing was left of the original, which is the sign it carried no information.

## Spanish post: recap with thanks

**Before**

```text
🚀 ¡Qué jornada increíble! 🚀

Ayer tuve el honor de participar en un evento que redefine el futuro de las
finanzas. La tecnología no es solo código — es confianza.

¿El resultado? Una audiencia inspirada y lista para el cambio.

✅ Innovación
✅ Comunidad
✅ Futuro

¡Súmate a la conversación! 👇

#Blockchain #Fintech #Innovación #Futuro #Web3
```

**After**

```text
Gran jornada ayer en [evento]!

Hablamos de cómo se usan hoy las stablecoins para pagos entre empresas, con
casos de Argentina y de la región.

Gracias [nombre] por la invitación y por armar un espacio para charlar de
estos temas con gente que los está construyendo.

Dejo el link al stream completo en el primer comentario 🦾
```

- **It starts from what happened** and says what was covered. The original said nothing a reader could repeat.
- **Thanks by name, for a concrete thing.**
- **The link goes in the first comment,** and the post ends there. No call for engagement.
- **One emoji closing a line** in place of emoji as headers and bullets. No hashtags.
- **Cut:** the contrast with the em dash, the self-answered question, the three-word list, "tuve el honor", and "Súmate" (neutral Spanish; his is "sumate").

## A review report

The shape of a review in this skill. Every finding has a rewrite, and the report stops there unless Tomi asks for the fix.

```text
### Must fix
1. Line 1: "Over 80% of firms lose clients to missed deadlines."
   → No source for the number. Give me one, or cut the line and open with
     line 3. (invented fact)
2. Lines 9-10: the ask ("can we meet Thursday?") is in the last paragraph.
   → Move it to line 2, right after the greeting. (answer buried)

### Should fix
3. Line 6: "It's not just a dashboard, it's peace of mind."
   → "You see every overdue filing in one list at 7 AM." (contrast frame,
     abstract claim)
4. No next step.
   → "If Thursday works, I'll send the invite today."

### Nice to have
5. Sign-off "Kind regards, Tomas Moreno" to someone he has met twice.
   → "Best," and "Tomi".
```
